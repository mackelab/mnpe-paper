# Authors

Who wrote the code in this repository, by experiment. The MNPE method itself is
implemented in [sbi](https://github.com/sbi-dev/sbi) and is not part of this code.

- **Jan Boelts** — the queueing ([queue](experiments/queue/)), coal-mining changepoint
  ([coal](experiments/coal/)) and scaling ([scaling](experiments/scaling/)) experiments; the
  toy example's budget study, baselines and sensitivity analysis ([toy](experiments/toy/));
  the ECE baselines and calibration diagnostics; the shared plotting style; and this
  repository.
- **Cornelius Schröder** — the first version of the toy example; the Hodgkin–Huxley
  experiment ([hh](experiments/hh/)), from simulator through inference, evaluation and its
  three figures; the discrete calibration plots, SBC plots and pairplot.
- **Jonas Beck** — the panels of Fig. 1, the method overview.
- **Daniel Gedon** — the toy example's analytical posterior and the discrete calibration
  (reliability diagram and ECE) function.

Jakob H. Macke, Michael Deistler and Daniel Gedon jointly supervised the work.
